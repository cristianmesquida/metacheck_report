This folder contains 10 example master's thesis written at HTI in 2025.

They were taken from "https://research.tue.nl/en/studentTheses/" on 10 August 2026, and filtered by Year and Department, which yielded 55 results.

I ran a random number generator to get 10 unique and random numbers between 1 and 55 which resulted in these ten numbers (sorted here): 1, 3, 8, 10, 22, 37, 38, 39, 46, 51

Theses were sorted by title (default), and according to above rule, the first, third, eighth, etc. theses were included in the final sample.
